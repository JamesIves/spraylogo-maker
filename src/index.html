    Fit

    GoldSrc sprays are a single image of up to 14,336 pixels. 64 × 64 works on every server.

    Compression makes flat, low contrast areas blocky, so a little extra contrast and sharpening helps.

    Auto uses DXT5 when the image has transparency. Uncompressed formats stay sharp but are four to eight times bigger.

    Cut-outs keep a little of their old background in their soft edges, which shows as a halo; removing halos cleans that up. Filling colour under transparent areas stops the game blending black or white into the edges.

    GoldSrc sprays hold 255 colours plus one transparent colour. Pixels below the cut-off become fully transparent.

    Far images

      Each image shows from its level onwards, until the next one takes over.

      Mipmaps are the smaller copies players see from further away. Use the mipmap slider under the preview to check each one.

      Timing
      Frames

      Sprays play at about 5 frames a second. Matching keeps the original speed by skipping frames. A frame limit of 0 keeps them all.

      Flags

      No Level Of Detail keeps the spray sharp on low texture settings. Clamping stops lines along the edges. Version 7.2 loads in every Source game.
